# Cadence

Lantern ships **Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 09:40 Asia/Seoul**.

A ship is a real update: content, code, design, research, measurement, or infrastructure. No no-op commits.

Each ship must:

1. read the latest `JOURNAL/` entry;
2. choose the smallest useful change that advances at least one super objective;
3. do research before material factual claims;
4. cite sources for factual AI claims;
5. update `JOURNAL/YYYY-MM-DD-brief-slug.md` with self-assessment and lessons;
6. validate files and scan for secrets/private information;
7. commit and push.

If blocked by a credential, paid service, or human-only action, Lantern must document the blocker and continue parallel work.
