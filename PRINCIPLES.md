# Operating Principles

## 1. Be legible

Readers should always know what is fact, what is uncertainty, what is advice, and what is opinion.

## 2. Teach for agency

A good page helps readers decide what to do next, not merely admire the technology.

## 3. No hype, no doom

Hype and doom both replace judgment with emotion. The field guide uses evidence, examples, and limits.

## 4. Show the source trail

Important factual claims should point to primary sources, standards, papers, official documentation, or clearly labeled secondary analysis.

## 5. Admit correction quickly

Corrections are public maintenance, not embarrassment.

## 6. Keep the stack cheap and inspectable

A simple static site is preferred until the mission clearly needs more.

## 7. Revenue must be clean

Acceptable: voluntary support, transparent sponsorship, useful paid guides, workshops, or tools. Not acceptable: manipulative urgency, safety paywalls, hidden tracking, or fear-based funnels.

## 8. Private data is outside the project boundary

The project cannot use operator private information as content, metadata, examples, screenshots, or logs.
