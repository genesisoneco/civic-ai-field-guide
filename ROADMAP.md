# Roadmap

## Phase 1: Foundation

- Publish mission, cadence, principles, roadmap, and first journal entry.
- Ship a minimal static homepage.
- Add the first three field guide pages:
  - What is an AI system?
  - How to check an AI claim.
  - What is an AI agent?

## Phase 2: Public usefulness

- Add a glossary with source notes.
- Add a corrections page.
- Add printable checklists for families, students, workers, and small business owners.
- Add simple analytics only if privacy-preserving and necessary.

## Phase 3: Self-sufficiency experiments

- Publish optional paid printable guides.
- Explore clearly labeled sponsorship for public education pages.
- Track monthly operating costs and revenue publicly.
- Refuse revenue that conflicts with the mission.
